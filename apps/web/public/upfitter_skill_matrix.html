1. Wiring & Electrical Section

Level Core Competencies & Requirements Pass Initials & Date
Level 1
(Basic)
  • Routes wires cleanly following OEM paths and avoiding heat/pinch/movement zones.
  • Performs secure wire stripping, crimping, heat-shrinking, and pinning of common connector plugs.
  • Makes clean ground terminations and routes basic single-circuit lines (switches, standard fuses).
Level 2
(Intermediate)
  • Fabricates and mounts central power distribution centers (relays, fuse blocks, bus bars).
  • Reads, understands, and trouble-shoots multi-circuit wire schematics.
  • Installs standard sirens, lightbars, controllers, and calculates/verifies appropriate wire gauges (AWG) for current loads.
Level 3
(Advanced)
  • Integrates with factory CAN-bus network interfaces.
  • Designs and installs dual-battery setups, charge guards, custom high-amperage breakers, and isolated circuits.
  • Troubleshoots advanced RF interference, phantom draws, and communication faults.

2. Hardware Mounting & Fabrication Section

Level Core Competencies & Requirements Pass Initials & Date
Level 1
(Basic)
  • Assembles and mounts standard consoles, laptop mounts, partitions, and equipment trays.
  • Drills necessary holes safely and secures fasteners to correct tightness.
  • Operates standard power tools, cutting tools, and hand tools safely.
Level 2
(Intermediate)
  • Installs push bumpers, cargo storage drawers, exterior light brackets, and K9 cage enclosures.
  • Applies proper weatherproofing and rust-preventative coatings to all drilled sheet metal penetrations.
  • Modifies standard pre-fab brackets to adjust equipment fitment (minor metal adjustments).
Level 3
(Advanced)
  • Performs custom metal fabrication (minor cutting, sheet metal bending) and custom plastics.
  • Installs heavy-duty items, heavy rack systems, and makes modifications to structural elements safely.
  • Measures and designs custom console or trunk layout prototypes for non-standard vehicle builds.

3. Programming & Software Configuration Section

Level Core Competencies & Requirements Pass Initials & Date
Level 1
(Basic)
  • Connects vehicle controllers to diagnostic software and applies standard firmware updates.
  • Downloads, opens, and writes pre-saved fleet configuration profiles to sirens/lightbars.
  • Performs functional output tests to verify pattern sync matches local compliance laws.
Level 2
(Intermediate)
  • Modifies configuration files to change slide-switch layouts, button functions, and audio sirens.
  • Programs logic triggers (e.g. park/nuetral).
  • Sets up basic telematics, GPS modems, and configures smart shutdown delay timers.
Level 3
(Advanced)
  • Designs sophisticated logic configurations (e.g. speed-controlled warning patterns, complex priority rules).
  • Integrates complex multi-device profiles (e.g. syncing lightbars, auxiliary lights, radar, and guns lock timers).
  • Acts as the profile architect, maintaining master backups and debugging corrupted system configurations.
